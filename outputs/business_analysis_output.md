# Glow Studio — Business Analysis

## 1. Business Overview

**Business Name:** Glow Studio
**Business Type:** Beauty Salon
**Location:** Hyderabad, Telangana, India
**Project:** AI Website Copy Generator for Local Businesses

Glow Studio is a beauty salon concept focused on personalised beauty services and customer care. The website aims to help visitors understand the available services and make appointment enquiries.

## 2. Target Audience

* People looking for professional hair styling.
* Customers seeking makeup for celebrations and special occasions.
* Individuals interested in skincare and facial services.
* Customers looking for nail care and nail styling.
* People who prefer a convenient way to enquire about salon appointments.

## 3. Services

The website showcases four main service categories:

1. Hair Styling
2. Professional Makeup
3. Skincare and Facials
4. Nail Care

## 4. Customer Needs

Potential customers need clear information about services, a welcoming salon experience, simple navigation, and an easy way to enquire about appointments.

## 5. Brand Voice

The brand voice should be:

* Friendly and welcoming.
* Professional and trustworthy.
* Elegant but easy to understand.
* Positive and confidence-building.
* Persuasive without making unrealistic promises.

## 6. Website Goals

* Introduce Glow Studio to potential customers.
* Communicate the salon's services clearly.
* Encourage visitors to explore services.
* Generate appointment enquiries.
* Make contact and booking actions easy to find.

## 7. Call-to-Action Strategy

The website uses clear action-oriented messages, including:

* Book Your Visit
* Explore Services
* Book This Service
* Send Booking Request
* Contact Glow Studio

## 8. Content Strategy

Website copy should use short paragraphs, clear headings, benefit-focused descriptions, and direct calls to action.

The content should be suitable for a local beauty salon and consistent across the homepage, services section, and booking section.

## 9. AI Tool

**Tool used:** Google Gemini

The business requirements and brand voice are used as context for generating consistent website copy through structured prompts.

## 10. Project Note

Glow Studio is the example business selected for this prompt engineering project. Business details and service descriptions should be verified before the content is used by a real salon.
