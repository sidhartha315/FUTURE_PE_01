# Glow Studio — Call-to-Action (CTA) Copy

## 1. Primary CTA

**Button Text:** Book Your Visit

**Supporting Text:** Ready for a little self-care? Contact Glow Studio to enquire about your appointment.

**Purpose:** Encourage visitors to take the next step towards booking a salon service.

## 2. Secondary CTA

**Button Text:** Explore Services

**Supporting Text:** Discover our hair styling, makeup, skincare and nail care services.

**Purpose:** Help visitors explore the salon's available services before contacting the business.

## 3. Service-Specific CTAs

| Service              | CTA                    |
| -------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Hair Styling         | Book Hair Styling      |
| Professional Makeup  | Book Makeup            |
| Skincare and Facials | Enquire About Skincare |
| Nail Care            | Book Nail Care         |

## 4. Contact CTA

**Heading:** Let's Plan Your Next Beauty Visit

**Message:** Have a question about our services? Get in touch with Glow Studio to discuss your preferences and enquire about an appointment.

**Button:** Contact Glow Studio

## 5. Booking Section CTA

**Heading:** Your Moment Deserves to Shine

**Message:** Take a little time for yourself. Contact us to enquire about available services and appointment options.

**Button:** Book Your Visit

## 6. Brand Voice

The calls to action use friendly, welcoming and professional language. They focus on helping visitors explore services and enquire about appointments without making unrealistic promises.
