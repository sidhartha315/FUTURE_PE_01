# Business Requirements Analysis Prompt

## Project

Future Interns — Prompt Engineering Task 1

## Objective

Analyze the business requirements for a local business before generating website copy.

## Prompt

Act as an expert website copywriter specializing in conversion-focused websites for local businesses.

Business Name: Glow Studio
Business Type: Beauty Salon
Location: Hyderabad, Telangana, India

Target Audience:

* Young adults
* Working professionals
* Local residents

Proposed Services:

* Haircuts
* Hairstyling
* Facials and skincare
* Hair treatments
* Personal grooming

Brand Voice:
Friendly, modern, welcoming, and professional.

Analyze the business requirements and identify:

1. Customer needs and expectations.
2. A clear business value proposition.
3. Key messages the website should communicate.
4. Suitable calls to action for bookings and enquiries.
5. The recommended writing style for the target audience.

Rules:

* Use clear, simple, professional language.
* Do not generate the complete website copy yet.
* Do not invent prices, reviews, awards, or business credentials.
* Treat Glow Studio as a demo business.

Output:
Present a structured business requirements analysis with clear headings.
