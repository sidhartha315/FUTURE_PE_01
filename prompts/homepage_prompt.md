# Homepage Copy Generation Prompt

## Project

Future Interns — Prompt Engineering Task 1

## Objective

Generate clear, benefit-driven homepage copy for a local business.

## Structured Prompt

**Role:**
You are a professional website copywriter specializing in conversion-focused content for local businesses.

**Business Details:**

* Business Name: Glow Studio
* Business Type: Beauty Salon
* Location: Hyderabad, Telangana, India
* Target Audience: Young adults, working professionals, and local residents
* Brand Voice: Friendly, modern, welcoming, and professional

**Task:**

Generate homepage copy containing the following sections:

### 1. Headline

Write an engaging headline that communicates the main customer benefit.

### 2. Subheadline

Explain who the business serves and what customers can expect.

### 3. Introduction

Write a short introduction describing the business concept, proposed services, and customer experience.

### 4. Calls to Action

Provide:

* One primary CTA encouraging bookings.
* One secondary CTA encouraging visitors to explore services.

**Content Requirements:**

* Use natural, customer-focused English.
* Make the copy specific to the business type.
* Avoid generic marketing clichés.
* Do not invent prices, ratings, reviews, awards, staff qualifications, or guarantees.
* Treat Glow Studio as a demo business.
* Do not claim unverified facilities or services.
* Make the content suitable for a professional website.

**Output Format:**

1. Headline
2. Subheadline
3. Introduction
4. Primary CTA
5. Secondary CTA
