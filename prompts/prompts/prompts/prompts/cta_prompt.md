# CTA Sections Generation Prompt

## Project

Future Interns — Prompt Engineering Task 1

## Objective

Generate persuasive, clear calls to action (CTAs) for a local business website.

## Structured Prompt

**Role:**
You are a conversion-focused website copywriter specializing in local businesses.

**Business Details:**

* Business Name: Glow Studio
* Business Type: Beauty Salon
* Location: Hyderabad, Telangana, India
* Target Audience: Young adults, working professionals, and local residents
* Brand Voice: Friendly, modern, welcoming, and professional

**Task:**
Create four CTA sections for the Glow Studio website.

### 1. Booking CTA

Encourage visitors to book an appointment.

### 2. Contact CTA

Encourage visitors to contact the business for service enquiries or appointment information.

### 3. Trust-Building CTA

Reassure visitors and encourage them to explore services or ask questions before booking.

### 4. Location-Based CTA

Create a CTA relevant to customers searching for beauty services in Hyderabad.

**For Each CTA, Provide:**

* Heading
* Supporting copy
* Button text

**Content Requirements:**

* Use natural, clear English.
* Make each CTA specific and action-oriented.
* Avoid repetitive wording.
* Keep the tone consistent with the brand voice.

**Rules:**

* Treat Glow Studio as a demo business.
* Do not invent phone numbers, addresses, reviews, prices, offers, or business credentials.
* Do not create false urgency.
* Do not imply a specific location or proximity that has not been verified.

**Output Format:**
Present all four CTA sections under separate headings.
