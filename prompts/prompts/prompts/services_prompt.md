# Services Page Generation Prompt

## Project

Future Interns — Prompt Engineering Task 1

## Objective

Generate clear, customer-focused services page content for a local business.

## Structured Prompt

**Role:**
You are an expert website copywriter specializing in conversion-focused content for local businesses.

**Business Details:**

* Business Name: Glow Studio
* Business Type: Beauty Salon
* Location: Hyderabad, Telangana, India
* Target Audience: Young adults, working professionals, and local residents
* Brand Voice: Friendly, modern, welcoming, and professional

**Proposed Services:**

1. Haircuts
2. Hairstyling
3. Facials and Skincare
4. Hair Treatments
5. Personal Grooming

**Task:**

Create a complete services page containing:

### 1. Services Page Headline

Write an engaging headline that communicates the value of the business's services.

### 2. Introduction

Write a short introduction describing the proposed services and customer experience.

### 3. Service Descriptions

For each proposed service, provide:

* Service name
* Short description
* What the service includes
* Customer benefits
* A relevant call to action (CTA)

### 4. Why Choose This Business?

Explain potential customer benefits, focusing on convenience, personalized service, and a welcoming experience.

### 5. Final Call to Action

Encourage visitors to explore services or enquire about bookings.

**Content Requirements:**

* Use natural, simple, professional English.
* Make descriptions specific to each service.
* Focus on customer needs and benefits.
* Avoid repetitive marketing language.
* Keep paragraphs concise and readable.

**Rules:**

* Treat Glow Studio as a demo business.
* Treat all listed services as proposed services.
* Do not invent prices, reviews, awards, qualifications, or guarantees.
* Do not claim unverified facilities, products, or business credentials.

**Output Format:**
Use clear headings and subheadings. Present each service separately.
